About & disclaimer

This app overlays OSHA sloping angles for soil Types A, B, and C on your live camera so you can visually compare a trench face. Ratios are horizontal:vertical (H:V).

  • Type A — ¾:1 (≈53°)
  • Type B — 1:1 (≈45°)
  • Type C — 1½:1 (≈34°)

Educational / field reference only. Not engineering advice. Not a substitute for a competent person, site-specific soil classification, protective systems, or applicable OSHA / local rules. Always follow your employer’s excavation safety program.

Tip: stand so the trench face fills the view. Drag the yellow baseline to the toe or crest, then scale until the guide matches the expected slope.
