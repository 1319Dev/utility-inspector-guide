About & disclaimer

Utility Inspector Guide is a mobile field toolkit: OSHA trench slope overlays, stamped photos, bell hole checklist, depth of cover, 811 checklist, trench card, pressure log, materials check-in, daily progress report, Station Locator (KMZ), Weather Radar (keyless NEXRAD + Blitzortung), Scope of Work search (PDF page highlights), and lookups.

Educational / field reference only. Not engineering advice. Not a substitute for a competent person, site-specific classification, protective systems, or applicable OSHA / local rules. Confirm clearances and cover mins against your company standards and codes. Compliance screens (projects, OQ, crew, Start of Day) are on-device records only — not ISNetworld, Veriforce, or Mitti API integrations. Always follow the operator SOW, approved procedures, and applicable regulation.

Install via Add to Home Screen for offline use after the first visit. Camera and motion need HTTPS.
